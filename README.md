**Coral Bay Heat** is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser — no downloads, no assets, no network. Everything is drawn on a canvas and every sound is synthesized with WebAudio: the city, the cars, the people, the story and the music are all made for this game, set in the sun-soaked coastal city of Coral Bay.

You arrive with $120, a pistol and a phone number: fixer **Mara Voss** has work. Her five-mission chain takes you across six named districts — Marina Mile, Old Cannery, Palm Heights, Portside Docks, Coral Flats and The Strand — delivering packages to Warehouse 9, lifting a coral Kestrel S for mechanic **Eddie Lark**, picking a fight with the rival crew **The Gulls**, out-running the clock on three drops, and making one last low-tide getaway while the whole Coral Bay PD is lit up behind you. Between jobs the city is yours: steal anything with wheels, hunt the 12 hidden packages, or just cruise the palm-lined beach as the teal water sparkles.

The law is a system, not a script. Crimes that get witnessed — theft, gunfire, collisions, worse — feed a heat meter that climbs from one star to five. Interceptors spawn at the edge of town and ram-hunt you; at four stars a helicopter joins with a swinging searchlight. Heat only cools while nobody can see you, and buildings genuinely block line of sight, so ducking down an alley works. Get caught standing still next to a cop and you are **BUSTED** (fine, and you walk out of Coral Bay PD); hit zero health and you are **WASTED** (St. Coral Hospital sends the bill). Traffic drives the road network and brakes for pedestrians, pedestrians stroll, panic and scream, and your cash, mission progress, packages and position save automatically.

Controls: **WASD / arrow keys** move and drive, **Shift** sprints, **E** enters and exits cars (occupied drivers climb out and flee), the **mouse** aims and **click or F** shoots, **Space** is the handbrake for drifts that lay real skid marks, and **Esc / P** pauses. The HUD carries your cash and health, wanted stars, a mission panel with live distance, a vehicle readout, and a working minimap with police blips and the gold objective marker.

## Run it

